## Status and precedence
These are agency operating policies, not legal advice or a signed contract. An executed proposal and agreement govern a specific engagement and may vary from these defaults.

## Inquiry and consultation
### Is the first call free?
Yes. The introductory remote call is approximately 20 minutes and is free. It covers the project goal, fit, rough scope, budget, and timing. Custom concepts, detailed audits, and workshops are paid work.

### How do I speak with an expert?
You may ask Ren to record a handoff request with contact details you consent to provide. The request is stored for later review. Recording a request does not mean that a staff member has been notified or guarantee a response time. The website also provides a Talk to an expert experience.

### When will someone reply?
The agency aims to respond within two business days during working hours, Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. Eastern Time, excluding US federal holidays. This is a target, not a guaranteed SLA.

### Can I visit the office or call a phone number?
Modo Studio operates from New York City and collaborates remotely. There is no public walk-in office address or general public phone line. Calls are arranged remotely by appointment after the team confirms the meeting.

## Quotes, payment, and fees
### Are website prices fixed?
Published fees are starting prices. The final quote depends on page count, content, technical requirements, research, integrations, stakeholder involvement, and deadlines. Taxes and external subscriptions/licenses are additional where applicable unless explicitly included.

### What is the payment schedule?
Fixed-fee projects under $5,000 use 50% before kickoff and 50% before final handoff or launch. Fixed-fee projects of $5,000 or more use 40% before kickoff, 30% at the approved design milestone defined in the proposal, and 30% before final handoff or launch. Monthly support and care plans are billed in advance. A signed proposal may specify a different schedule.

### How can I pay?
Invoices may offer ACH/bank transfer or a secure card payment link when configured. Do not share card numbers, bank login details, or payment credentials over email or chat.

### When are invoices due?
Invoices are due within seven calendar days of issue unless otherwise agreed. The kickoff payment must clear before work starts. Overdue payments may pause work and delay delivery. No automatic late-fee percentage is specified here; refer billing disputes to the team.

### Can I get a discount or payment plan?
There is no standard automatic discount. A phased scope or custom payment schedule can be discussed with an expert. Reducing deliverables is an available discussion point; do not expect the same scope to be delivered for a lower fee.

### Is the deposit refundable?
The kickoff payment is applied toward the project fee. Cancellation is reconciled against work completed and approved non-cancellable commitments. Any unused prepaid balance is refunded; additional amounts may be due for work already performed. It is not automatically fully refundable or automatically fully forfeited.

## Scope and revisions
### What is a revision round?
A round is one consolidated set of client feedback on a defined deliverable, followed by one update by the studio. Feedback should come from the appointed decision-maker and include all stakeholder comments together. A new brief, new direction after approval, added deliverables, or replacement content that requires redesign may be a scope change rather than a revision.

### How many revisions are included?
Brand strategy: two rounds on the strategy draft. Visual identity: two rounds on one selected direction. Brand foundation: two strategy rounds and two selected-identity rounds. Landing pages: two visual-design rounds and one pre-launch correction review. Marketing websites and design-only websites: two wireframe rounds and two visual-design rounds; builds also have a pre-launch correction review. Product UI/UX: two wireframe and two visual-design rounds. Marketing kits and presentations: two rounds on the agreed scope. Monthly support uses purchased hours rather than an unlimited revision allowance.

### Can we change scope midway?
Yes, subject to a written change estimate. We describe the added fee and schedule impact before starting the change. The standard out-of-scope estimate rate is $150/hour; larger additions may use a fixed fee.

### Do you provide sample designs before a contract?
No custom speculative design is included in the free introductory call. Work begins after the agreement, cleared kickoff payment, required inputs, and confirmed start date.

## Schedule and client responsibilities
### When can a project start?
Start dates depend on capacity and are confirmed by the team. Published durations describe project length, not the next available opening.

### What do you need from us?
An appointed decision-maker, approved content, brand assets if available, licensed imagery, required technical access, and timely consolidated feedback. Use secure invitations to platform accounts rather than sending passwords via email or chat. The client supplies legally appropriate privacy, terms, and cookie text; the studio does not draft legal policies as part of standard design work.

### How quickly should feedback be provided?
We ask for consolidated feedback within three business days of a review request unless the project plan states otherwise. Missing content or late approvals can move the delivery date. Silence does not automatically mean acceptance.

### What if we need to pause?
Notify the team. If required client inputs are missing for ten consecutive business days, the project may be paused and rescheduled according to capacity. Restart timing and any additional work are confirmed in writing; no automatic restart fee is specified. A delay beyond 30 calendar days requires a revised schedule and scope review, not automatic forfeiture of the entire payment.

### Can you meet an urgent deadline?
We assess rush work individually. Accepted rush projects generally carry a surcharge starting at 25% of the affected fee. No deadline or rush slot is promised before confirmation.

## Ownership, assets, and portfolio
### Who owns the final designs?
Under the standard agreement, rights to the agreed original final deliverables transfer after full payment, subject to the signed contract. Unselected concepts, internal working material, pre-existing methods/components, and third-party assets are not automatically transferred. Editable file delivery is defined by the service and proposal.

### Will I receive source files?
Identity projects include the agreed final vector and raster logo exports. Website and product design projects include agreed Figma handoff. Presentation and marketing deliverables use the editable format agreed at kickoff. Website builds are delivered to the client's platform account where applicable. Internal explorations and every working file are not standard deliverables.

### Are fonts and stock images included?
Paid font, stock, plugin, and software licenses are excluded unless the proposal states otherwise. The client must obtain licenses appropriate for its use. We can recommend options but cannot guarantee third-party licensing terms without checking them.

### Can you guarantee that my logo is trademark-safe?
No. Naming, trademark clearance, and registration require separate specialist review.

### Can you show our work publicly?
The default agreement requests permission to display final public work after launch. Confidential work, embargoes, NDAs, and exceptions must be agreed in writing.

## Website delivery and support
### Do you provide hosting and a domain?
We can help configure them within the agreed scope, but accounts and recurring charges belong to the client. Hosting, domains, paid plugins, and platform plans are not included in starting website fees.

### Do you build ecommerce or custom applications?
These require a separate assessment and proposal. Standard marketing website packages do not include checkout, inventory, customer accounts, custom backends, or complex business integrations.

### Do you make accessible websites?
We consider contrast, readable type, keyboard interaction, semantic structure, and accessible form labels within the agreed implementation. We do not promise formal accessibility certification or legal compliance. A formal audit or specialized remediation is separately scoped.

### Which devices and browsers do you test?
Standard website QA covers current stable Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge at delivery, plus representative mobile and desktop viewport sizes. Testing every device, legacy browser, or third-party embedded widget is not included. Exact coverage can be agreed in the proposal.

### What happens after launch?
Website builds include 30 calendar days of correction for reproducible defects in the delivered, agreed implementation, beginning at handoff. New content, new features, client changes, third-party outages, and updates outside the original scope are excluded. Report an issue with the page URL, steps, device/browser, and screenshots without exposing sensitive information. No guaranteed emergency response time is included.

### What is the website care plan?
The plan starts at $250/month and includes up to two hours of routine website updates, small content changes, and basic checks within the agreed platform. Hours do not roll over; extra work requires written approval at $150/hour. It excludes redesigns, hosting charges, major migrations, security guarantees, and 24/7 incident response. Billing is monthly in advance, with no three-month minimum. Cancel with 30 calendar days' notice before renewal. Platform-specific maintenance duties are defined in the care agreement.

### How is ongoing design support different?
Design support starts at $2,000/month for up to 15 hours and has a three-month initial term. It supports a prioritized design queue with one active task at a time. Coordination, calls, and revisions count toward hours. Unused time does not roll over. Website care and third-party subscriptions are not automatically included.

## Cancellation and refunds
Fixed-fee project cancellations must be requested in writing. The team documents completed work and approved non-cancellable costs, reconciles these against payments, and identifies any remaining balance or refundable unused amount. Deliverable ownership follows the agreement and payment status. No automatic refund-processing timeline is stated; billing confirms the actual timing.

Ongoing design support may be cancelled effective after its initial three-month term with 30 calendar days' notice before renewal. Website care may be cancelled with 30 calendar days' notice before renewal and has no three-month minimum. Services continue through the paid period unless otherwise agreed. If a monthly service is stopped early at the client's request, unused reserved capacity is not automatically refundable. Studio non-delivery or a dispute requires individual review.

## Privacy and handling information
Collect only what is needed for an inquiry or project. We ask permission before recording contact details for follow-up. Do not share passwords, card information, identity documents, patient records, or other sensitive customer data in an inquiry.
