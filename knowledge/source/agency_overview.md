## Who we are
Modo Studio is an independent design agency based in New York City. We help ambitious businesses develop clear brands and useful digital experiences. Our work combines brand strategy, visual identity, website design and development, and product interface design.

Our philosophy is simple: make the business easier to understand and the experience easier to use. A strong identity should translate consistently across the website, product, marketing materials, and everyday customer interactions. We favor purposeful typography, clear hierarchy, considered color, and accessible interfaces over decorative complexity.

We work remotely with clients throughout the United States and internationally. New York is our operating base, not a public walk-in office. We do not publish a street address or offer unscheduled in-person visits. Any in-person meeting is subject to a separately confirmed arrangement.

## Who we work with
Our typical clients include early-stage companies preparing to launch, established small and midsize businesses updating their positioning, service businesses improving their websites, and teams developing a new digital product. Projects are assessed by fit, objectives, scope, and budget rather than by industry alone.

A good fit is a client who can explain the business problem, appoint a decision-maker, provide timely feedback, and commit to a defined scope. We can help clarify an early idea through paid discovery, but a free introductory call is not a complete brand strategy workshop.

We do not have a fixed minimum company size or geographic restriction. Projects involving sensitive data, complex regulated workflows, or specialist technical requirements need additional review. We do not claim expertise or certifications that are not listed in this knowledge base.

## What we do
- Brand strategy: audience, positioning, messaging direction, and creative direction.
- Visual identity: logo systems, typography, color, supporting graphics, and guidelines.
- Websites: strategy, wireframes, responsive interface design, and development within an agreed scope.
- Product UI/UX: user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity screens, and interactive prototypes.
- Marketing design: launch assets, presentation decks, social templates, and campaign graphics.
- Ongoing design support: monthly capacity for teams needing repeated design work.

Website projects may use Webflow or WordPress depending on requirements. The platform is selected during scoping. Custom application development, complex ecommerce, backend engineering, and third-party systems are separately assessed; they are not included automatically in a standard marketing website.

## What we do not offer as standard services
We do not sell pre-made logos, provide unlimited revisions, or operate a 24-hour design desk. Printing, photography, video production, legal advice, trademark registration, paid-media management, and ongoing SEO campaigns are not standard inclusions. We can discuss coordination with a client's specialists, but do not promise a partner referral or subcontractor availability without confirmation.

We do not guarantee revenue increases, conversion lifts, rankings, accessibility certification, or trademark availability. Design decisions can support business goals, but outcomes depend on implementation, traffic, offer, and market conditions.

## Our process
### 1. Introduction
The prospective client submits an inquiry or requests a free 20-minute introductory call. We discuss the business, desired outcome, approximate scope, target launch date, and budget. The call is a fit check and does not include free custom designs.

### 2. Discovery and scoping
We identify audiences, content requirements, deliverables, dependencies, technical constraints, and review responsibilities. Complex projects may begin with paid discovery from $1,000. We explain whether discovery is separate from, or credited toward, a subsequent project in the proposal; credit is not automatic.

### 3. Proposal and agreement
The proposal defines deliverables, exclusions, fees, payment milestones, estimated schedule, revision allowance, and acceptance criteria. Work begins after the agreement is signed, the first payment clears, required inputs are received, and a start date is confirmed.

### 4. Strategy and design
We develop the agreed direction and deliver work in reviewable milestones. Clients provide consolidated feedback through one appointed decision-maker. Standard packages include two revision rounds per specified review milestone.

### 5. Build or production
For website projects, approved designs move into implementation. Product-design engagements move into prototype refinement and developer handoff rather than production software development unless separately contracted.

### 6. Quality review and delivery
We review the agreed deliverables, resolve in-scope issues, and prepare handoff. Websites receive responsive and functional checks within the stated test coverage. Final files and launch/handoff are released after final payment.

### 7. Support
Completed website builds include a 30-calendar-day defect correction period beginning at handoff. Ongoing maintenance, new features, and content updates require a separate agreement.

## Communication and working hours
Our standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. Eastern Time, excluding US federal holidays. Eastern Time follows daylight saving time; use America/New_York when interpreting dates. We aim to respond to project messages within two business days, but this is not a guaranteed response SLA.

Active projects use email, a shared project workspace, and scheduled video calls. Weekly status updates are standard during active work. More frequent meetings or additional stakeholder workshops may change the scope.

## Starting a project
You may ask Ren to record a handoff request with contact details you consent to provide. The request is stored for later review; it does not mean that a staff member has been notified or guarantee a response time. The website also provides a Talk to an expert experience.

Helpful inquiry details are name, preferred contact method, company or project name, service of interest, business goal, approximate budget, desired launch date, and existing website URL if applicable. Do not share passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or confidential customer records in your initial inquiry.

An introductory call is free and lasts approximately 20 minutes. Calls take place remotely by appointment.
