## How to interpret pricing
Prices below are starting project fees, not instant quotes or guaranteed maximums. A written proposal establishes the final scope and fee. Applicable taxes, hosting, platform subscriptions, domains, paid assets, and third-party licenses are excluded unless explicitly listed. Tax treatment is confirmed during invoicing.

Standard work beyond an approved scope is estimated at $150 per hour and requires written approval before it starts. Package timelines are estimated elapsed calendar weeks from a confirmed kickoff, assuming timely feedback and complete inputs. They are not immediate availability promises. Packages cannot simply be added together to calculate a bundled price or schedule; combined engagements receive a separate proposal.

## Brand strategy sprint — from $2,500
For businesses that need clearer positioning before investing in design.

Includes one remote discovery workshop of up to 90 minutes; review of client-supplied business materials; a focused review of up to five competitors; audience priorities; positioning statement; key messaging pillars; and two visual moodboard directions. Delivery is a concise strategy document and one selected creative direction.

Includes two consolidated revision rounds on the draft strategy document. It excludes logo design, naming research, trademark checks, large-scale customer research, a complete copywriting system, and website production.

Client inputs include the business offer, target customers, goals, existing materials, and one decision-maker. Strategy research is directional and does not replace a statistically representative market study.

## Visual identity — from $4,500
For a business with an agreed name and sufficiently clear positioning.

Includes a discovery session; two initial visual identity directions; refinement of one selected direction; primary logo, secondary arrangement, and simple brand mark where appropriate; typography and color system; supporting graphic direction; and a concise brand guideline document.

Final delivery includes agreed SVG, PDF, PNG, and JPG logo exports, color values, type recommendations, and a guideline PDF. Two revision rounds are included on the selected direction. Developing additional concepts after direction selection is a scope change.

Excludes strategy sprint deliverables, naming, trademark registration, custom typeface design, packaging, printing, animation, and website design. Font licenses and paid stock assets are client costs unless specified.

## Brand foundation — from $6,500
Combines the defined brand strategy sprint and visual identity scope into one coordinated engagement. Includes the workshop, strategy document, two moodboard directions, two initial identity directions, development of one selected identity, final logo assets, typography/color system, and guidelines.

Includes two revision rounds at the strategy review and two at the selected identity review. The lower combined starting fee reflects coordinated scope; it is not an automatic discount for every combination of services. Typical duration is 5–7 weeks. Website work is separate.

## Single landing page design and build — from $3,000
Includes one responsive landing page of up to eight content sections, a wireframe, one visual direction, implementation in the agreed platform, one basic inquiry form, standard page metadata, and deployment to the client's configured account/domain.

Includes two revision rounds on the visual design and one consolidated pre-launch correction review. Client supplies approved copy, brand assets, imagery, and required legal text. A confirmation screen or simple form thank-you state is included; additional standalone pages are not.

Excludes ecommerce, member accounts, complex integrations, full copywriting, paid-media management, analytics strategy, and hosting subscriptions. A landing page is a single page, not a five-page website package.

## Marketing website design and build — from $7,500
Includes up to five main marketing pages, commonly Home, About, Services, Work, and Contact. Page names are examples and can be adjusted within scope. Includes a content planning session, sitemap, wireframes, responsive design, development in Webflow or WordPress as agreed, one standard contact form, basic on-page SEO setup, and handoff training of up to 60 minutes.

Includes up to one CMS collection with an agreed schema and up to ten initial entries supplied by the client. A collection template and entries are separate from the five main marketing pages. Additional collections and entries require an estimate.

Two revision rounds are included at the wireframe milestone and two at visual design. Implementation follows approved designs. One consolidated pre-launch review covers defects and alignment with approved scope; it is not an additional redesign round.

Basic SEO setup means agreed page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, sitemap configuration where supported, and image alt text based on supplied content. It excludes keyword campaigns, backlinks, ranking guarantees, and ongoing optimization.

The client owns platform accounts and supplies hosting, domain access, approved copy, privacy/cookie text, and asset licenses. Advanced forms, CRM automation, multilingual content, ecommerce, complex animations, custom applications, and legal-policy drafting are excluded.

## Website design only — from $4,500
Includes sitemap, wireframes, and responsive Figma designs for up to five main pages, plus a small reusable component set and design handoff notes. Includes two wireframe revision rounds and two visual-design rounds. Delivery is design documentation, not a deployed website.

Development, platform setup, QA of a third-party developer's implementation, content migration, and hosting are not included. We can scope an implementation review separately.

## Product UI/UX starter — from $5,000
For one clearly defined feature or a focused early product experience. Includes a discovery workshop, up to two core user flows, low-fidelity wireframes, up to ten unique high-fidelity screens, a starter UI component library, and a clickable Figma prototype.

A screen is a distinct layout/state agreed during scoping. Mobile adaptations, error states, dashboards, and additional flows can add scope; ten screens does not mean unlimited variants. Includes two consolidated wireframe revision rounds and two visual-design rounds.

Excludes application development, authentication/backend implementation, production-ready code, recruitment of research participants, paid usability studies, and a full enterprise design system. Deliverables support developer handoff; they do not constitute a functioning software product.

## Marketing launch kit — from $1,800
Requires an existing identity. Includes six reusable social-media templates, two digital campaign/banner designs, one email-header graphic, and a short usage guide. One initial design direction and two consolidated revision rounds are included.

Templates are delivered in the agreed editable format, typically Figma or Canva. Publishing, social account management, advertising spend, photography, and ongoing content creation are excluded.

## Presentation design — from $1,500
Includes visual design for up to 12 slides using finalized client-supplied content and an existing identity, plus one title-slide direction and two consolidated revision rounds. Delivery format is agreed before work, such as PowerPoint or Google Slides.

Investor strategy, financial modeling, substantial copywriting, research, custom illustration, and animation are separately scoped. Editable output does not include licenses for any third-party font or asset.

## Paid discovery — from $1,000
For complex or unclear engagements. Includes one workshop, a requirements summary, key constraints, recommended workstreams, and a preliminary delivery plan. It does not include final design or implementation. Any credit toward a later project must be explicitly stated in the proposal.

## Ongoing design support — from $2,000 per month
Includes up to 15 hours of reserved design capacity per month, one active task at a time, a prioritized task queue, and one monthly planning call of up to 30 minutes. Work may include marketing assets, presentations, website design updates, and product interface work within team capabilities.

This is an hours-based engagement, not unlimited design. Calls, coordination, revisions, and production work count toward the 15 hours. Unused hours expire at month end and do not roll over. Additional time requires written approval at $150 per hour. Minimum initial term is three months, with monthly billing in advance. Subsequent cancellation requires 30 calendar days' notice before the next renewal.

Task estimates depend on complexity and available monthly capacity. There is no universal one-day turnaround promise. Hosting, software subscriptions, and website maintenance are not automatically included.

## Rush work
Rush requests are evaluated against current capacity. If accepted, a surcharge generally starts at 25% of the affected project fee and is stated in the proposal. Payment of a surcharge does not create availability unless a rush schedule is confirmed.
